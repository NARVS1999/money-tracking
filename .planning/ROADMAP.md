# Roadmap: Money Tracking

## Overview

A personal expense/income tracker for the phone (Expo Go workflow): sign in with email/password, log entries in under 10 seconds with session-scoped offline persistence, manage per-type categories, view the current-month summary, and export date-range PDF/Excel/CSV summaries to the phone.
