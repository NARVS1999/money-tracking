<objective>
**As a** user, **I want to** sign in with my email and password and reach my empty app shell — with the session remembered across restarts and a clear inline error on wrong credentials **so that** my private ledger is protected from the very first launch.

Purpose: This plan delivers the observable AUTH behaviors of the phase on top of the 01-01 skeleton: the AuthProvider context (single source of truth for auth state), the error-mapping unit (AUTH-03), the UI-SPEC Sign In screen, the loading gate, and the 5-tab shell. The gate swaps SignIn <-> MainTabs purely from onAuthStateChanged state — no manual navigation anywhere.

Output: A working sign-in flow with persistent sessions, inline credential errors, and the full text-only tab shell.
</objective>
